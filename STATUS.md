# NDH‑TIDS Status

This repository is active but in early consolidation. 
Documentation across NDH repos was created under duress and may be incomplete, 
fragmented, or conceptual. NDH‑TIDS is now established as the topological 
binding layer, but full integration with NDH‑CORE, Serenity Manifold, and 
Orbital systems is pending.

A unified roadmap will be added only after triage and stabilization.
