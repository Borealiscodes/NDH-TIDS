# **3 — Traditional Multimodal Alignment Characteristics**

### **3.1 — Embedding Fusion**
Multimodal systems typically rely on:

- CLIP‑style joint embeddings  
- cross‑modal attention matrices  
- semantic fusion layers  
- graph‑based reasoning (GAT, cross‑modal graphs)  

These are powerful but **data‑dependent** and **drift‑prone**.

### **3.2 — Drift Mitigation**
Stability is achieved through:

- fine‑tuning  
- contrastive learning  
- alignment losses  
- multimodal consistency training  

These reduce drift but cannot eliminate it.

### **3.3 — Post‑Hoc Correction**
When multimodal hallucination occurs:

- classifiers  
- rerankers  
- filters  
- post‑processing  

attempt to correct it.

This is reactive.
