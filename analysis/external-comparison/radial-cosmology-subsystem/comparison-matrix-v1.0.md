### 1. Purpose

- **Yours:** Designed as both a **story‑born cosmology** and a **general radial rendering engine**. It’s explicitly meant to take *any structured domain* (mythic, organizational, cognitive, etc.) and express it as a governed radial constellation.
- **Others:**  
  - Cosmology tools focus on **physical universe modeling** (CMB, dark matter, cosmic web).  
  - Mythic frameworks focus on **conceptual mapping of myths**, not geometry.  
  - Radial graph layouts focus on **data visualization**, not cosmology or narrative.  
  - Radial graphics engines focus on **aesthetics**, not structure or governance.

Your subsystem’s purpose is **hybrid**: visualization + narrative + architecture + governance. That combination doesn’t show up elsewhere.

### 2. Geometry model

- **Yours:**  
  - Concentric **rings** (Central, Mountain, Story, Meta).  
  - Deterministic **angles and radii**.  
  - Explicit **Cartesian coordinates**.  
  - Geometry tightly bound to **node classes** and **graph topology**.
- **Others:**  
  - Cosmology tools use **physical coordinates** (3D space, comoving distance, etc.).

Your geometry is **semantic radial geometry**: the rings *mean* something, not just “where nodes go.”

You’re occupying a rare space: **narrative cosmology with formal geometry and graph logic**.
